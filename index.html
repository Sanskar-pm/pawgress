Why do traditional trackers fail?

People don't fail because they're lazy. They fail because the experience feels cold.

Sterile Checklists

Traditional apps are transactional. Checking a box provides a fleeting dopamine spike that quickly loses value when motivation wanes.

The Drop-off Cliff

Shame-Based Streaks

Losing a 30-day streak induces guilt. This "failure state" causes massive Day-30 churn. Users abandon the app entirely to avoid the shame.

Cold Utility

Current tools rely 100% on intrinsic discipline. When life gets overwhelming, a spreadsheet won't encourage you to try again tomorrow.
