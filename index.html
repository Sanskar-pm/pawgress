Solving the Retention Cliff

Traditional trackers suffer massive D30 drop-off. Losing a 100-day streak induces shame, leading to app abandonment. Pawgress replaces this with Soft Loss Aversion. Breaking a streak makes your pet sleepy, triggering an empathetic response to return, rather than guilt.

Target Audience & Personas

Pawgress appeals to distinct behavioral segments by balancing aesthetic appeal, gamified accountability, and zero-guilt mechanics. Understanding these resonance points drives our product design and feature prioritization.

📚

The Overwhelmed Student

Wants to romanticize study sessions. High anxiety, prone to doom-scrolling. Needs a digital "best friend." High propensity for cosmetic microtransactions.

💼

The Burnout Professional

Overwhelmed by corporate Jira boards. Needs low-pressure tracking for work-life balance. Prime candidate for the Pawgress Pro subscription.

🧠

The ADHD "Sprint" Tracker

Struggles with executive dysfunction and object permanence. Requires ambient accountability (widgets) and high visual rewards. High brand loyalty.
